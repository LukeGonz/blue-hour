The Concept

Twenty minutes. Every evening.

Blue hour lasts twenty minutes. The sky holds a blue that doesn't exist at any other time -- saturated, still, slightly electric. Blue Hour was built around that window. A restaurant for the people who notice it.

Dinner begins at dusk. The room is designed to hold that specific quality of light for as long as possible. By the time it's fully dark, you've already decided to stay.
